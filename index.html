BookTrak

Catalogue data and cover images from Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive. Open Library is free, keyless and openly licensed, which is why it is the primary source here and not a fallback.

Google Books is optional and only consulted when you supply your own API key — anonymous access to it is closed · Editions and covers are contributed by Open Library's own volunteers, so corrections belong upstream · A personal, non-commercial tool.
